Planning the first classroom trial of MitoMapa

Classroom trial protocol · web 4.8.12 · Editable resource 4.8.7 · 25 October 2026.

Status: Classroom trial with learners pending. Use this sheet to check whether learners understand the resource and can use it with the planned support. Trial results and evidence of educational impact are still pending.

Before the session

  1. Agree on a short route. If it suits the group, start with one island and a session of about 45 minutes. Choose an aim, a task and a way to share the work. Teachers link the activity to the curriculum for their group and choose the stage.
  2. Try the route. Open the story and its challenges, listen to an excerpt and check the Workshop. If you plan to compare a source with the story, prepare a short source. Check headphones, connectivity and the option to listen without background sound. If using the ELPX, check that it opens in your installation.
  3. If you include the island names game, try dragging and a non-drag alternative using touch, a click, the keyboard or the accessible form. Check that learners understand the cautions about historical names and that the map is a modern recreation. They can continue at any point in the game. Plan the support learners will use. Choose a text size, supported reading, pauses and a way to respond by speaking, writing, drawing or performing. Check the keyboard controls and the tools the group will use. Record any limits you could not test.

During the session

Note the time each phase actually takes and any instructions you need to explain. Check that learners can distinguish the agreed route from the collection of pieces, find their way back to the story and take part with the available support. Record what help you offered without grading or ranking learners.

If learners get stuck, offer an alternative and continue. Ask a brief question to see if they can distinguish a fictional detail from information supported by a source. The illustrations are modern recreations.

After the session

  1. Discuss what was clear, what made it hard to continue and what could change. You can download voluntary feedback comments without completing a rating scale.
  2. Review the agreed evidence and record completion of the route when appropriate. Assess the work with learners. Save a local progress file if you plan to continue on another device.
  3. Choose one improvement to prioritise and decide how to check it in the next session. A positive comment or a correct answer alone cannot establish learning or educational impact.

Notes without learner identifiers Leave out names, initials, email addresses, images, voices, device identifiers and extracts of work that could identify learners. Summarise difficulties and support in general terms. Keep this sheet and individual progress records local.

Use the browser’s Print option to print the observation sheet on its own. Fill it in on paper.
